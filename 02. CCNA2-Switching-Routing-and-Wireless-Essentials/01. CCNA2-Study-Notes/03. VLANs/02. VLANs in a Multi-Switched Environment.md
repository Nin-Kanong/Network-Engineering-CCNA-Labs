## Defining VLAN Trunks

**VLAN Trunk** is a connection between network devices that carries traffic from multiple VLANs over one physical link.

### How does it work?


<img width="1666" height="617" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e0e18cb3-7d37-4c61-a0a0-16775e516f83" />


- S1, S2, and S3 are connected using trunk links.
- Trunks carry VLAN 10, 20, and 30 traffic between switches.
- Devices in the same VLAN can communicate even when connected to different switches.

---


## Network with VLAN & Without

| Without VLAN                          | With VLAN                               |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| One broadcast domain                  | Multiple broadcast domains              |
| Devices share one LAN                 | Devices separated into logical networks |
| Less network segmentation             | Better security and organization        |
| Broadcast traffic reaches all devices | Broadcast traffic stays within its VLAN |

> VLANs divide one physical network into multiple logical networks.
